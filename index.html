Introduction

Yang Liu (刘阳)

Associate Professor @ Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU)

liuy856@mail.sysu.edu.cn
School of Computer Science and Engineering, SYSU, Guangzhou, China
Core Member of HCP-Lab

I am currently an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU), and a core member of the HCP Lab. I obtained my Ph.D. degree from Xidian University in 2019. My research primarily focuses on Embodied AI & Robotics, Self-evolving Agents, and Multimodal Perception & Reasoning.

I have published 60+ papers in top-tier venues including TPAMI, TIP, TKDE, CVPR, ICCV, ICML, NeurIPS and ACM MM — among them 7 first/corresponding-author journal papers were selected as ESI Highly Cited / Hot Papers, 5 conference papers as Oral / Highlight, and one ranked Top-2 on the TMech Popular Articles list. I co-authored the monograph Multimodal Large Models: The New Paradigm of Artificial General Intelligence (10,000+ copies sold, adopted as a textbook by 20+ universities; English edition published by Springer, 2026). As PI, I lead 10+ projects funded by NSFC (General / Young / Key sub-project), Pengcheng Laboratory, Huawei, and provincial foundations. I serve as an Associate Editor of Pattern Recognition, as associate editor-in-chief of the group standard Technical Requirements for Multimodal Perception and Pattern Recognition of Embodied Intelligence, and as a co-initiator of the self-evolving embodied agent operating system PhyAgentOS.
